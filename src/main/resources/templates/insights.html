Inference & Insights

AEGIS watches every decision Qusar's agents make, so it can see what no single decision shows: an agent that changed behaviour, a region treated worse than others, confidence that doesn't predict correctness, and the lines of business where AI is helping — or costing — the carrier. Every number comes from a classical, explainable statistical test. No black-box model and no LLM.
