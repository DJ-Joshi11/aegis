AEGIS (AI Exposure Governance & Insurance Shield) is an AI-exposure-and-coverage evidence mapper that sits alongside Guidewire's Qusar agentic AI framework. It ingests the decision evidence Qusar agents already produce on top of PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter-style workflows, flags the ones that need compliance review under the right jurisdiction's rules, maps every flagged (or unreviewed) decision to a broad insurance exposure category, and exports a structured, timestamped audit evidence trail a human compliance or underwriting reviewer can act on.

What AEGIS explicitly does not do: certify compliance, make a legal coverage determination, or set a premium. Qusar and Guidewire's own systems already provide or are developing agent-level governance controls — AEGIS doesn't replace that. It makes the AI decisions those controls act on visible, categorized by exposure, and auditable.

Process specification

What each AEGIS stage actually does, mapped to the real Guidewire touchpoint it depends on.

01 Decision Ingestion LIVE
Guidewire touchpointA Qusar agent issues a decision on top of a PolicyCenter (underwriting) or ClaimCenter (claims) transaction — that decision event is what AEGIS ingests.
TriggerPOST /api/decisions — either a real agent feed calling this endpoint, or (today, no sandbox access) MockDecisionGenerator calling the same code path on a schedule.
AEGIS actionValidates required fields, generates a decision ID/timestamp if missing, persists an immutable audit row independent of Guidewire's own logs, then publishes it in-process for detection to react to.
Why it mattersYou can't build an exposure/coverage evidence trail for a decision that was never recorded outside the agent's own black box. This is the system of record everything else depends on.
ImplementationFully built. MockDecisionGenerator emits decisions in the exact shape a real Qusar feed would use, so swapping in a live feed only touches this one class.
02 Detection & Compliance LIVE
Guidewire touchpointRuns against every decision regardless of which Guidewire product generated it — same rule engine for an underwriting call and a claims call.
TriggerIn-process Spring event, fired synchronously the instant ingestion persists a decision — no polling delay.
AEGIS actionTwo rules: (1) any FRAUD_FLAG decision outcome FLAGGED always routes to review; (2) an agent whose DENIED rate crosses 50% over a 5+ decision sample gets its latest denial flagged as a statistical outlier. A flagged decision is tagged with the disclosure standard its jurisdiction calls for (EU AI Act, UK FCA, IRDAI, or a US state rule) — a tag for a human reviewer to check against, not a certification that the decision satisfies it.
Why it mattersRegulators increasingly require human-reviewable explainability for automated decisions. Manual review can't keep pace with agent volume — this catches what needs a second look without slowing the agent down.
ImplementationFully built and rule-tested (4 unit tests covering clean, always-flag, rate-outlier, and below-threshold cases). Rules are intentionally simple, demonstrative logic — not a certified compliance engine.
03 Evidence Mapping LIVE
Guidewire touchpointStands downstream of every Guidewire-triggered decision, turning AEGIS's own governance findings into evidence rather than a claims payment or any other automated action.
TriggerTwo independent paths: a detection flag creates an evidence record immediately, tagged with the decision's exposure category; a scheduled sweep (every 10s) catches any decision whose compliance review sat unresolved past a 45s review window and records it as a control gap anyway.
AEGIS actionMaps the decision type to a broad insurance exposure category (Underwriting Risk, Claims Handling, or Fraud & Integrity Exposure), persists an evidence audit row with the category and reason, and publishes the result.
Why it mattersAEGIS doesn't pay a claim or certify anything — it makes sure every AI decision that needed a second look actually produces evidence a human can review, instead of disappearing into the agent's own logs.
ImplementationFully built, unit-tested, and the review-gap sweep path was verified end-to-end running locally.
04 Exposure Aggregation LIVE
Guidewire touchpointAnalogous to a risk-visibility report a carrier's own compliance team would compile by hand — AEGIS automates the roll-up, not the decision that follows from it.
TriggerRecalculated on every evidence outcome reported by the evidence mapper, per agent.
AEGIS actionTracks each agent's decisions-vs-evidence ratio and its dominant exposure category, exposed at /api/exposure-summary, plus a downloadable audit bundle at /api/evidence/export.
Why it mattersA carrier can't manage AI exposure it can't see. Surfacing which agents generate the most audit evidence — and in which exposure category — is the differentiated, feasible slice of the original pitch: visibility and evidence, not a parallel insurance product.
ImplementationFully built and unit-tested. Deliberately produces no premium figure or coverage term — see the scope banner above.

Run it yourself

This submits a real decision to the running backend via POST /api/decisions — not a canned animation. Because every stage above runs synchronously in-process, the full result (flagged or not, evidence recorded or not, which exposure category) is back within a fraction of a second.

Tip: pick SIU Fraud Referral with outcome FLAGGED to see an instant compliance flag and evidence record — that rule always fires. The denial-rate outlier rule needs a run of history from the same agent, so it's easier to catch live in the ambient feed on the Decision Feed page than from a single manual submission.
