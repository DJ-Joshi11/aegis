AEGIS (AI Exposure Governance & Insurance Shield) is a governance-and-claims layer that sits alongside Guidewire's Qusar agentic AI framework. It audits every autonomous decision a Qusar agent makes on top of PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter-style workflows, flags the ones that need compliance review under the right jurisdiction's rules, pays out automatically when the agent or the review process fails the policyholder, and feeds each agent's real track record back into what it costs to insure that agent's decisions.

Process specification

What each AEGIS stage actually does, mapped to the real Guidewire touchpoint it depends on.

01 Decision Ingestion LIVE
Guidewire touchpointA Qusar agent issues a decision on top of a PolicyCenter (underwriting) or ClaimCenter (claims) transaction — that decision event is what AEGIS ingests.
TriggerPOST /api/decisions — either a real agent feed calling this endpoint, or (today, no sandbox access) MockDecisionGenerator calling the same code path on a schedule.
AEGIS actionValidates required fields, generates a decision ID/timestamp if missing, persists an immutable audit row independent of Guidewire's own logs, then publishes it in-process for detection to react to.
Why it mattersYou can't govern or insure a decision that was never recorded outside the agent's own black box. This is the system of record everything else depends on.
ImplementationFully built. MockDecisionGenerator emits decisions in the exact shape a real Qusar feed would use, so swapping in a live feed only touches this one class.
02 Detection & Compliance LIVE
Guidewire touchpointRuns against every decision regardless of which Guidewire product generated it — same rule engine for an underwriting call and a claims call.
TriggerIn-process Spring event, fired synchronously the instant ingestion persists a decision — no polling delay.
AEGIS actionTwo rules: (1) any FRAUD_FLAG decision outcome FLAGGED always routes to review; (2) an agent whose DENIED rate crosses 50% over a 5+ decision sample gets its latest denial flagged as a statistical outlier. A flagged decision is routed to the compliance standard for its jurisdiction (EU AI Act, UK FCA, IRDAI, or a US state disclosure rule).
Why it mattersRegulators increasingly require human-reviewable explainability for automated decisions. Manual review can't keep pace with agent volume — this catches what needs a second look without slowing the agent down.
ImplementationFully built and rule-tested (4 unit tests covering clean, always-flag, rate-outlier, and below-threshold cases). Rules are intentionally simple, demonstrative logic — not a certified compliance engine.
03 Payout Engine LIVE
Guidewire touchpointStands in for a ClaimCenter-style indemnity payment, but triggered by AEGIS's own governance findings rather than a human adjuster.
TriggerTwo independent paths: a detection flag fires payout immediately ($250); a scheduled sweep (every 10s) catches any decision whose compliance review sat unresolved past a 45s SLA window and pays it anyway ($300 — worse outcome, higher payout).
AEGIS actionPersists a payout audit row with the amount and trigger reason, publishes the result, and reports the outcome to the pricing engine.
Why it mattersAEGIS isn't only a governance layer, it's a coverage product: if the agent misfires or review is too slow, the policyholder is made whole automatically instead of waiting on a manual claims process.
ImplementationFully built, unit-tested, and the SLA-sweep path was verified end-to-end running locally.
04 Pricing Feedback LIVE
Guidewire touchpointAnalogous to a PolicyCenter rating factor — except the "insured" is the AI agent itself, not a policyholder.
TriggerRecalculated on every payout outcome reported by the payout engine, per agent.
AEGIS actionTracks each agent's decisions-vs-payouts ratio and derives a premium multiplier, exposed at /api/pricing.
Why it mattersStatic governance rules go stale. Tying an agent's real error rate to what it costs to insure that agent's future decisions makes the incentive to stay clean economic, not just procedural.
ImplementationFully built and unit-tested. Not yet wired to a real underwriting/rating engine — see the feedback note in the diagram above.

Run it yourself

This submits a real decision to the running backend via POST /api/decisions — not a canned animation. Because every stage above runs synchronously in-process, the full result (flagged or not, paid out or not, repriced or not) is back within a fraction of a second.

Tip: pick SIU Fraud Referral with outcome FLAGGED to see an instant compliance flag and payout — that rule always fires. The denial-rate outlier rule needs a run of history from the same agent, so it's easier to catch live in the ambient feed on the Decision Feed page than from a single manual submission.
