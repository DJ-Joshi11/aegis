Two ways evidence gets created

Trigger ADetection flag0

Detection publishes a flagged decision; the evidence mapper records it immediately with its category and the reason it was flagged.

Trigger BReview-gap sweep0

Safety net: every 10s a sweep finds decisions still unresolved after the 45s review window and records them as a control gap — so a slow or failed review never leaves a decision without a trail.
